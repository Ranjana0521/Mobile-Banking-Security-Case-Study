Standard OTPs are vulnerable to transfer-tampering (e.g. user thinks OTP is for ₹500, but attacker changes amount to ₹5,000). SecureBank cryptographically computes:

HMAC_SHA256(OTP_KEY, Salt || OTP || User || Recipient || Amount || Timestamp)

Any variation in recipient or amount renders the OTP cryptographically invalid.
