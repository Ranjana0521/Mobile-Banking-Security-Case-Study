THREAT MITIGATION MAPPING

Addressing the Three Core Threat Vectors

How the proposed engineering and cryptographic solution neutralizes attacks defined in the case study.

THREAT VECTOR 01

Mobile Malware Defense

Protects against banking trojans (SharkBot, TeaBot), malicious accessibility services, and screen tap-loggers.

  • Anti-Keylogger Scrambled Keypad: Dynamically randomizes numeric coordinates to blind touch-logging malware.
  • RASP Integrity Attestation: Detects debugger attachment, Frida instrumentation hooks, and root exploits.
  • Anti-Overlay Shielding: Prevents unauthorized floating overlay windows from hijacking PINs or OTP inputs.
THREAT VECTOR 02

Anti-Phishing & Spoofing

Defeats fake banking portals, DNS poisoning, cloned smishing links, and credential-harvesting phishing kits.

  • Mutual Visual Secret Phrase: A server-held personal emblem and passphrase displayed exclusively on authentic sessions.
  • SSL/TLS Certificate Pinning: Validates SHA-256 fingerprint of the bank's public key, preventing MITM proxies.
  • Real-Time Link Analyzer: Real-time heuristic scanning of URLs for typosquatting, Punycode, and suspicious TLDs.
THREAT VECTOR 03

Unauthorized Transactions

Mitigates account takeovers, unauthorized fund transfers, payload tampering, and replay attacks.

  • Dynamic HMAC-SHA256 OTP: MFA token bound to recipient + amount; cannot be replayed for another transaction.
  • AES-256-GCM AEAD at Rest: Encrypted with GHASH authentication tags; any bit-flip tampering triggers immediate rejection.
  • Adaptive Risk Scoring Engine: Real-time screening of transaction velocity, high-amount anomalies, and device trust.
